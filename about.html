About Al Tawafah

Company Overview

Setting Standards in Corporate Maintenance & Manpower

Al Tawafah was established with a singular vision: to create an organization that provides unparalleled support services for businesses, industries, and homes. Today, we handle millions of square feet of facilities and oversee a workforce of hundreds of professionals.

We operate as a single-point solution, removing the administrative bottlenecks of hiring, background-checking, and managing specialized trades. From factory-floor cleaning protocols to intricate HVAC mechanical installations, our clients focus on their business while we keep the facility secure and operational.

Our Mission

As a comprehensive building maintenance company, we prioritize customer satisfaction above all else. We offer eco-friendly equipment and non-toxic products to guarantee clean, healthy, and safe environment for our clients across MEP, plumbing, electrical and civil work.

Our Vision

To deliver the highest possible quality of technical and maintenance services at the lowest possible price and at the fastest possible time, building 360-degree adaptability in residential and commercial sectors.

Values That Drive Us

Our Core Values

We foster a professional culture built on safety compliance, customer satisfaction, and respect for our team members.

Absolute Safety

We hold safety above metrics. Every individual is trained to recognize, reports, and stop unsafe acts immediately.

Operational Excellence

We maintain rigid performance SLAs, utilizing modern equipment and continuous training workshops.

Client Satisfaction

Our high quality of work is measured by the satisfaction of our clients, delivering top-tier technical solutions.
