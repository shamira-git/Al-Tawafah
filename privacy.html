Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

1. Introduction

At Al Tawafah (Facilities & Manpower), we value the confidentiality and privacy of our visitors, clients, and career applicants. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, share, and protect information when you navigate our static website or fill out our online quote and recruitment forms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you explicitly submit to us. This includes:

  • Contact Form Data: Name, email address, telephone number, company title, and service details requested.
  • Careers Form Data: Name, contact details, position selected, cover message, and resume files (PDF, DOCX).
  • Automated Data: Anonymized cookies, browser types, operational device details, and site navigation paths collected to analyze website speed and theme toggling parameters.

3. How We Use Your Information

Al Tawafah utilizes collected metrics solely for operational business pipelines. Specifically to:

  • Compile custom facility management or manpower quote proposals.
  • Review candidate credentials for active trade career vacancies.
  • Deliver emergency system updates for clients with active Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).
  • Save theme configuration switches (light/dark layout selection) on your local browser database.

4. Information Safety & Retention

We do not sell, rent, or lease your private information to third-party marketing services. Collected data is encrypted during submission and accessible only by authorized operations managers, compliance heads, or HR recruiters. We store contact and career records only as long as necessary to fulfill service agreements or evaluation loops.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your region, you have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of the data we hold. If you have questions or would like to request file removals, contact our safety and compliance office at altawafah-technical@hotmail.com.

Al Tawafah is a leading provider of premium maintenance, facility management, and professional manpower solutions. We empower residential, commercial, and industrial clients with skilled support.
