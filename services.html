FAQ

Common Service Queries

Get quick answers regarding contract durations, deployment schedules, and emergency support policies.

What is the typical lead time for supplying manpower?

For standard positions, we can supply workers within 3 to 5 business days. For large-scale unskilled labor (over 50 helpers), we request 7 to 10 days notice to ensure proper safety onboarding and document validations.

Are all deployable personnel insured by Al Tawafah?

Yes. Al Tawafah operates under 100% legal compliance. Every helper, technician, and engineer is covered by Workers' Compensation Insurance and General Liability policies, shielding your firm from claim liabilities.

Can we customize an AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract)?

Absolutely. Our AMCs are highly customizable. You can include specific checks (e.g. only HVAC and Electrical switchgear) or request all-inclusive packages containing plumbing, civil work, and regular deep cleaning.
